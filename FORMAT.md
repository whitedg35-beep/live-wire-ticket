# LIVE WIRE TICKET™ — Format Specification

The public shape of the format. (Build machinery is not published.)

## Canvas

- Vertical video, 1080 × 1920.
- The ticket face is built like a real ticket, in landscape, centered on
  the vertical canvas over a blur-fill of itself.

## The face

Required elements:

- Masthead: **THE DAILY WIRE** / **VICE CITY WIRE**
- The headline — the face states what the piece is (the article IS the
  ticket face)
- **GOLDEN TICKET** designation and the ticket number: **No. NNNN**
- Perforation line and a notched **ADMIT ONE** stub
- Barcode on the stub
- Microprint line: `VALID FOR ONE PERSON - DOORS 7PM - SHOW 8PM - NO REFUNDS`
- Color canon: pink on the left edge, teal on the right edge, gold foil
- Schemes: **FULL GOLD** (crinkled foil) or **MARBLE WHITE** (white,
  gold foil, pink/teal marble veining through the interior)

## The play (timing)

1. **Face** — about 2 seconds, slow push-in, hype sound under it
   (drumroll energy).
2. **Wire-in** — white-flash cut (~0.35s) straight into the content.
   The transition reads as amperage: the ticket live-wires into the reel.
3. **Content** — runs in full.
4. **Back** — about 2 seconds: `THE WIRE CARRIED IT LIVE`, the ticket
   number, `FOLLOW @vicecitywire92`, and `PASS THIS TICKET ON`.

## Animation note

The face arrives **crinkled, then opens** — the foil ticket uncrinkles /
opens up into its flat, readable form before the wire-in.

## Numbering

Tickets are numbered sequentially, four digits (`No. 0002`). A number is
issued once, to one piece. The number appears on the face and the back.

## The Token

For surfaces a ticket cannot fit (round marks, avatars, coins, small
badges): a gold coin — **the Token** — carrying the LIVE WIRE TICKET™
name and the pink/teal edge colors as enamel inlay. The Token is the
same mark in coin form, under the same license.

## Banners and surfaces

Any banner-shaped surface in the house runs ticket style: the banner IS
a wide ticket — masthead, headline, perforation, stub, number. Any
surface that can hold a ticket gets one, with the issuer's name on it:
the house is the one handing them out.
