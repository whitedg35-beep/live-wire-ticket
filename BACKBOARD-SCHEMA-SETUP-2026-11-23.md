# THE BACKBOARD — Schema & Setup (Nov 23, 2026)

Set by Garrett Whited. The backboard is the one board everything plugs into. It carries its own schema and setup, the ticket sits on top, and a ticket is matched to every board.

## 1. What it is

- The SURFACE in the plug-in contract: **SURFACE (the backboard) → SCHEME MASK (ours) → PIECE (content)**.
- Every build starts from a **blank copy of the backboard** — never on the base board itself.
- One board per house, struck from the same template; the house's scheme mask and registry entry change, the board's anatomy does not.

## 2. Anatomy (Top → Bottom) — 1080×1920, 30 fps video-ready

1. **THE TICKET ON TOP** — a landscape LIVE WIRE TICKET™ face laid across the head of the board: GOLDEN TICKET designation, masthead, headline slot, perforation + notched ADMIT ONE stub, decorative barcode, microprint (VALID FOR ONE PERSON - DOORS 7PM - SHOW 8PM - NO REFUNDS), pink left edge #ec4899 / teal right edge #14b8a6, gold foil trim.
2. **THE COIN SLOT** — one recessed circular medallion seat under the ticket, sized for the house Token (1:1 master, radial alpha). The coin lands here, spins here, opens here.
3. **THE PIECE WINDOW** — one large gold-framed window under the coin slot. The content plays inside it (the 7-beat opener ends with the video playing in this window).
4. **THE REGISTRY RAIL** — eight small coin seats along the base, No. 0001–No. 0008, the house's own seat lit, the rest dim. The whole board is always on the board.
5. **THE LICENSING BASE** — footer strip: © 2026 Bumkus, licensed, never sold · the disclaimer link · the house handle/domain.

## 3. The Matched Ticket Rule

- **Every board is matched with its ticket.** The ticket on top carries the board's number and the piece's name; the board is not complete, and does not go out, without its ticket on top.
- Ticket and board share the number: Board No. {NNNN} ↔ Ticket No. {NNNN}.
- The ticket is the front door; the board is the house behind it.

## 4. Canvas & Colors

- Canvas: matte charcoal-black #0a0b0f, subtle #ec4899 left / #14b8a6 right ambient rim glow.
- Scheme masks per the Exact Schema §1 (wire-pink-teal / full-gold / marble-gold-silver / whited-silver-brass). The mask dresses the board; the anatomy never changes.
- House registry, emblems, inlays, finishes: Exact Schema §2 (No. 0001–No. 0008).

## 5. The Outbound Gate (applies to the board like everything else)

- Before anything goes out, the licensing is in the piece AND the disclaimer link rides with the send: https://github.com/whitedg35-beep/live-wire-ticket/blob/main/LICENSE
- A board with no ticket on top does not ship. A piece with no licensing does not ship.
