## 2. The 2-Minute Real Foley Kit (Phone Recording)

Record 5–6 clean, dry takes in a quiet room so every sound in the cuts is 100% ours:

- **The Coin:** Spin a heavy coin or brass token on wood, slate, and glass (capture both the spin whirr and the final wobble-settle).
- **The Paper & Foil:** Unfold a real newspaper sheet, snap a stiff card, and uncrinkle foil by hand.
- **The Wire-In Switch:** Click a real mechanical wall switch, camera shutter, or brass key tap for the 0.35s flash cut.

## 3. Lining Up the 7-Beat Coin-Opener

- Beats 1–2 (0.0s–1.2s): Coin on Daily Wire page (soft wood thud) → shadow sweep (quiet paper air-brush).
- Beats 3–4 (1.2s–2.4s): Coin drops into place (crisp tap) → rolls off (spinning whirr).
- Beats 5–7 (2.4s–3.5s+): Gold-to-marble turn (stone click) → opens (foil/paper snap) → video plays inside while the tension bed drops cleanly underneath.

When the raw phone clips land, cut and gain-stage them per segment straight onto the builds (ai-office-token-spin.mp4, ai-office-spin-and-paper.mp4, and the creator openers).
